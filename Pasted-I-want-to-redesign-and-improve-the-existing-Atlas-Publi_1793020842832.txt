I want to redesign and improve the existing Atlas Publication website, making it a sophisticated, modern, editorial-style website for an international cultural and literary publication.

**IMPORTANT: Preserve the existing Atlas logo, current colour palette, typography where appropriate, and overall visual identity. Do not replace the logo, introduce a new colour scheme, or rebuild the website unnecessarily. Work within the existing React/Vite project and improve the current design, navigation, content and functionality.**

The website should resemble a professional independent cultural magazine, with elegant typography, spacious layouts, thoughtful imagery, subtle animations and a polished editorial aesthetic. Avoid making it look like a generic university society website or corporate landing page.

## 1. HOMEPAGE — ATLAS

The main slogan should be:

**"Where Cultures Connect"**

Make this the prominent headline on the homepage, underneath or alongside the Atlas logo.

Underneath, include a short introduction:

"Atlas is a student-led publication exploring the cultures, stories and perspectives that shape our world. From personal reflections and literary expression to travel, identity and global conversations, we bring together voices across borders, celebrating both what makes us different and what connects us."

The homepage should feature:
- A beautiful, editorial-style hero section with the slogan and a short introduction.
- A prominent "Explore Atlas" button that takes readers to the publication's main content.
- A "Latest Stories" section featuring recently published articles, with article images, titles, categories, author names and publication dates.
- A "Notes From Around the World" section with visually appealing cards linking to the different regional categories.
- A featured creative writing section introducing "Margins".
- A "Join Our Contributors" section encouraging students to write for Atlas.
- A clean footer with navigation links, social media links, contact information and the Atlas logo.

Make the homepage visually engaging without overcrowding it.

## 2. NOTES FROM — REGIONAL PUBLICATIONS

One of the main navigation sections should be called **"Notes From"**.

When a visitor hovers over or clicks "Notes From", a dropdown menu should appear displaying the following regions:

- Notes From the Americas — covering North America, Central America, South America and the Caribbean.
- Notes From Europe
- Notes From Asia
- Notes From MENA — Middle East and North Africa
- Notes From Sub-Saharan Africa
- Notes From Australia & Oceania

Each region should have its own dedicated page where readers can explore articles, essays, cultural commentary, personal experiences and perspectives related to that part of the world.

Each regional page should have:
- A beautiful regional heading, e.g. "Notes From Asia".
- A short, thoughtful introduction to the region's section.
- An editorial article grid with featured images, titles, authors and publication dates.
- Space for articles covering culture, traditions, society, history, identity, contemporary issues and personal reflections.
- Article filtering or tags where useful.

Ensure that "Notes From" is itself a clickable main page, showing articles from all regions, alongside the regional dropdown.

These regions are editorial categories, not restrictions on who can contribute. Writers should be able to explore their own experiences and perspectives, including cross-cultural and diasporic identities.

"Not every story fits neatly within the lines. Margins is Atlas's home for creative expression — a space for poetry, fiction and the words that resist categorisation. Here, we invite writers to explore memory, identity, belonging, imagination and everything in between."

Below the introduction, include a short section titled "What belongs in Margins?" with three categories:

**Poetry**
Original poetry in any style, exploring personal experiences, cultures, emotions, identity and imagination.

**Short Stories**
Original works of fiction, whether inspired by lived experiences, cultural traditions or entirely imagined worlds.

**Literary Prose**
Creative reflections, flash fiction and experimental writing that blur the boundaries between genres.

Each category should have its own filter or subsection, allowing visitors to browse creative work easily.

Display submissions in an elegant literary-magazine format. Poetry should be particularly readable, with generous spacing and careful preservation of line breaks.

Include a "Submit to Margins" button linking to the Join Us page.

## 7. ADDITIONAL FEATURES

Please also implement the following:

**Article Pages**
Create a consistent, professional article layout with:
- Article title and subtitle.
- Author name and publication date.
- Category and regional tags.
- Optional cover image.
- Readable article text with clear paragraph spacing.
- Author biography where available.
- Related articles at the bottom.

**Search and Discovery**
Add a functional search feature so readers can find articles by title, author, region or topic. Include appropriate filtering options.

**Mobile Responsiveness**
Ensure that the entire website works beautifully on phones, tablets and desktops. Dropdown menus should become accessible mobile navigation menus.

**Editorial Consistency**
Use the existing colour palette throughout. Maintain consistent spacing, typography, button styles, image dimensions and article-card designs.

**Content Management**
Structure the website so editors can easily add, edit and remove articles, update commissions and manage editorial-team profiles without needing to redesign pages. Use reusable React components and a centralised content structure. Do not introduce a paid CMS or unnecessary dependencies without asking first.

**Accessibility**
Ensure sufficient colour contrast, accessible navigation, descriptive image alt text, keyboard-friendly menus and semantic headings.

**Performance**
Keep the website fast and lightweight. Use subtle hover effects and smooth transitions without excessive animation.

The overall impression should be that of an independent, international literary and cultural magazine — refined, thoughtful, creative and welcoming.

Prioritise strong editorial layouts, meaningful whitespace, striking but restrained imagery and excellent readability.

Avoid generic corporate illustrations, unnecessary gradients, oversized buttons or excessive decorative elements.

Retain the current Atlas logo and existing brand colours exactly.

Do not remove existing published content or break existing working functionality. Reuse existing articles and assets where possible, and preserve existing routes or provide redirects when changing URLs.

Please implement these changes in the existing project, rather than only describing them. Review the current codebase before making edits, and ensure the website builds successfully afterwards.
